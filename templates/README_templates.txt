These example EC-Lab .mps templates were included in the uploaded FlowBot archive.
They are not generated from scratch by this repository packaging step.
Review their technique parameters and compatibility with the local Bio-Logic EC-Lab
software, its OLECOM registration, and the target potentiostat before live use.
The original templates contain generic C:\BioLogic_OLE_Test paths in their Filename
headers; biologic_runner.py rewrites this header when it generates a run .mps file.
Do not treat these templates as verified or approved experimental protocols.
